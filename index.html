Personal Profile

The story behind the work.

I'm a 3D animator and designer with hands-on experience across 3D animation, illustration and brand identity work. I'm currently studying for an MSc in User Experience at Birmingham City University, layering a research-driven UX mindset on top of a creative production background.

Along the way I've picked up real-world experience in customer-facing and social-media roles — which sharpened my communication, adaptability, and instinct for what an audience actually responds to. I bring all of that into every project I touch, whether it's a character animation, a brand identity, or a digital product.
