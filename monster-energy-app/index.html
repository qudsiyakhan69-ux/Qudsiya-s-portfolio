What it does

Four reasons to open it.

Energy drinks get bought on impulse, so the brand rarely hears from the people drinking them daily. This concept turns the can itself into the way in.

Scan & earn

Scan the code under the ring pull for 50 XP. Levels run from Rookie to Legend, and Pro unlocks drops an hour early.

Flavour drops

A live countdown to each limited release, with a notify button and a queue of people waiting for the same can.

Events & stock

Gaming, skate and moto events nearby, plus which shops actually have your flavour chilled right now.

Caffeine in check

Every scan logs against the 400mg daily guide, with a ring that turns red rather than hiding the number.

The screens

Twelve screens, one loop.

First open, pick what you're into, scan a can, earn XP, spend it, and come back for the next drop.

The look

Black, so the cans glow.

Near-black backgrounds let the product do the talking, and Monster Green is kept for one thing per screen: the action worth taking. Flavour colours come straight off the cans, so the Ultra Red page feels like the drink in your hand and the Wild Passion drop turns the whole screen purple.

Designing it responsibly

Rewards that don't push another can.

A loyalty app that pays people to drink more caffeine is an easy thing to get wrong, so the loop that hands out XP also keeps count.

A visible limit

The tracker ring turns red past the 400mg daily guide instead of hiding the number.

Capped rewards

Three scans a day maximum, so XP can't be farmed by drinking more.

Nudges you control

"Nudge me after 2 cans" and "No caffeine reminders after 6pm" are the drinker's settings, not the brand's.

Honest labelling

The splash screen carries the high-caffeine warning and the concept disclaimer.
